qud-caves-caaaw

I've been playing this game called Caves of Qud.
Let me explain this screenshot cuz it's wild.

This game is a roguelike in the "classic" sense. (See games like Brogue)
So the entire game world is a flat grid with very simple graphics, and ALL of the gameplay is turn-based. Nothing happens unless you take some action (like moving, attacking, casting a spell, resting, etc). You can always plan out your next move.

The main window is the game view. The top left of that window shows my character standing in a cave, near the stairs that led down from the floor above. The torch I'm holding lights up some of the tiles. On the right is a log of stuff that happened over the past few turns. There's info about a fight that just occurred, involving a cannibal and a skunk. Skunk's still present, in the lower-right portion of the area lit by my torch.

So here I'm inspecting a club dropped by a cannibal that I finished off after it stumbled out of a cloud of bright green acid emitted by the skunk it attacked. The acid has killed some cave flora and melted a hole in the shale cave wall.

The club I'm inspecting is painted.
When the cannibal was swinging it at me, I assumed this just meant it was red or green or orange or something,
but no!

The last few entries in the event log on the side show my character taking note of a historic site I've just learned about, and some info about the ancient sultan Polyxerhim IV. I've even received a quest to go visit the site.
This all happened because the club is painted with a whole scene from the life of this sultan. The scene gets an entire paragraph in the object description. Down at the bottom you can also see that this club would give me +40 reputation with the Flock of the Wife to Caaw-caaaw-caaw.

I have no idea what this means! But it sounds like a good perk to get from the painted club I looted from the skunk acid-weakened cannibal I finished off. This lil chunk of painful wood just exudes history and import I guess. It should probably go in a museum, but I might need to hit a skunk with it instead.



This game has some unique vibes.
What I've seen so far of the setting and general world lore is fun, and the writing style for all the in-game descriptions is interesting:

The setting has that eclectic sci-fantasy blend going on where the world has been around long enough for civilization to rise and fall long ago, so now you can have your magic robot villages or whatever. Swords and magic and bows ..and guns and lasers and genetic mutations. You find a weird artifact in a cave and the local village tinker identifies it for you as ..a battery cell. You pay him in freshwater, because apparently the apocalypse made the world extra salty. No but like extra extra salty.

The main hesitation I have right now is run length..
Cuz so far it plays like a whole big RPG only I can't save-scum and there's permadeath.
I am multiple hours into my first run right now and feel like I haven't scratched the surface of basically anything!
So it might be pretty devastating to die in this game.

They do have an alternative gamemode called "Roleplay" mode where you're allowed to save at settlements though, so if the permadeath gets annoying I guess I'll switch to that to experience the whole main questline.
Apparently the main quest is good!
