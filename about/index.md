# About Me

_Hello!_  
I'm Isaac K.  

I'm a software engineer, currently contributing to the [Open Decision Intelligence](https://opendi.org/) initiative as a standards architect and software engineer. The goal is to help grow the DI industry that enables people to _engineer_ decisions with intent, connecting [actions](https://opendi-org.github.io/glossary/#action) to [outcomes](https://opendi-org.github.io/glossary/#outcome). You can find more details on my [projects page](/about/projects/#opendi-initiative), and read my [What is Decision Intelligence](/blog/what-is-decision-intelligence) post for a DI primer.

On my blog, I (occasionally) write about technical topics, music I'm listening to, or whatever else I feel like.  
For a list of all my blog topics, see [the tags page](/blog/tags).

I set up a publicly-browsable [personal photo gallery](/gallery) as a way to get me to engage with my old photos more. There are descriptions, titles, tags, and plans for interactive filtering, scores, and alternate views so I can have more fun browsing it. Have a look!

I live in a smallish town in Virginia with my partner. We'd like to move to the west coast ASAP! Currently considering Portland, Seattle, (PNW in general,) or the SF Bay area.
